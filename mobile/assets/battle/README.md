# HoloCity park plate

Prompt: Remove both robots and every UI element from the reference; reconstruct the uninterrupted sunny grassy park and futuristic HoloCity skyline. Preserve the stylized game art, white towers with cyan details, blue sky, trees, rocks, low 0.9 m camera and 35 mm lens. Keep an empty grassy foreground for live 3D robots, subtle cyan arena perimeter arcs, portrait composition with the park horizon near the middle. No characters, text, interface, borders or watermark.

The generated PNG was converted to JPEG at quality 86 for the app. This is a painted backdrop, not a Unity terrain export or live city geometry.
